How fee drag is calculated

Each year the portfolio earns the return before fees, then the fee is charged on the grown balance, then the contribution is added. Fees paid are summed year by year. The cost of the higher fee is the gap between the two ending balances: the extra fees themselves plus the growth those dollars would have earned.

Using this with clients

  • Show the dollar cost of a fee difference, not just the percentage.
  • Compare an advisory fee against the value of planning, tax work and behavioral coaching, which this calculator does not measure.
  • Run a withdrawal scenario (a negative contribution) for clients already in retirement.

What this calculator does not cover

  • Tiered or breakpoint fee schedules, taxes, and returns that vary year to year.
