How the gap is calculated

Everything is in today's dollars, using the real return (the return after inflation). The nest egg needed is what it takes, at retirement, to pay the income goal minus Social Security and pensions every year until the plan-to age, rising with inflation. Projected savings grow the current balance and yearly savings to retirement. The gap is the difference, and the extra savings figure is the yearly amount that closes it by retirement.

What this calculator does not cover

  • Taxes on withdrawals, which raise the gross amount the nest egg must provide.
  • Return sequence risk, one-off expenses, and savings that grow with salary.
  • Social Security that starts at a different age from retirement.
